Accessibility Statement

Last updated July 28, 2026 · Reviewed annually
Privacy Terms Accessibility Disclaimer All policies

Nadir Douglas / SITSOL is committed to making nadidoug.com and hosted projects accessible to the widest possible audience.

Conformance target

WCAG 2.2, Level AA is the target standard for the main portfolio site. Individual experimental demos (for example, WebGL and 3D experiences) may not fully conform; where that is the case, an accessible alternative or description is provided.

Measures taken

  • Semantic HTML and correct landmark and heading structure
  • Keyboard navigability and visible focus indicators (WCAG 2.4.11–2.4.13)
  • Color contrast meeting AA ratios, verified against the site palette
  • Alt text for informative images; decorative images marked appropriately
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected in scroll-driven and animated experiences
  • Form labels, error identification, and target sizes per WCAG 2.5.8

Known limitations

  • Interactive 3D viewers rely on pointer interaction; a keyboard-accessible fallback is planned.
  • Third-party embeds may not meet AA; alternatives are available on request.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, email nadi@nadidoug.com with the page URL and a description of the issue. The target response time is 3 business days. This statement is reviewed annually.
